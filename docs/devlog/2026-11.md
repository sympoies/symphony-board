# Development log - 2026-11

## 2026-11-27 - GitLab resolve retries query-validation timeouts

### Result

- A GitLab per-item resolve that fails with 'Timeout on validation of query' is
  retried after 2 and 6 s before the item fails. The retry-delay helper is now
  provider-neutral (transientRetryDelaysMs).

### Why / context

- One item's validation timeout turned a whole full sync partial and failed
  deploy gates that need a clean full sweep. Reporting the sweep complete with
  a failed-items list was ruled out. The failed item's notes, and so its
  note-derived edges, are unknown, and a complete full sweep would retire those
  edges. After exhaustion the source therefore stays partial.

### Evidence

- New tests failed before the change (one attempt per resolve). They now show
  one timeout retried to a complete sweep, persistent timeouts failing only
  that item after three attempts, and other GraphQL errors not retried.

## 2026-11-27 - Ingest freshness ignores future-dated activity for lag

### Result

- Polled activity dated after the route's observed_at now counts as activity at
  observed_at, and the response reports it in activity_warnings
  (future_activity, invalid_activity_timestamp).

### Why / context

- Source timestamps up to an hour ahead of receipt produced lag_exceeded and
  503 while Live was receiving. Dropping future rows was ruled out: the
  per-repo bounds keep only the maximum, so a stalled receiver would then look
  healthy. Clamping to observed_at caps the lag at the time since Live last
  ingested, which is a real measurement.

### Evidence

- Five new evaluator and route tests failed on the unchanged evaluator and pass
  after the change. They cover future-only and mixed activity, a working and a
  stalled receiver, the observed_at boundary, and unparseable timestamps.

### Links

- <https://github.com/sympoies/symphony-board/issues/883>
